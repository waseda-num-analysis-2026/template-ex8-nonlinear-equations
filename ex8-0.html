2. Handout Study with AI Tutor

Goal

While reading the shared Lectures 7–8 handout, insert at least 3 Q&A blocks of your own — questions you actually had while reading, answered with the help of an AI (GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, etc.) following the protocol in AI_TUTOR.md.

Target: focus on Section 4 (multi-dimensional Newton’s method) and Section 5 (simultaneous polynomial root-finding by the DK method), which are the topics for Lecture 8 and Exercises 8.1–8.2. Questions about any other section of the handout are also accepted.

Steps

  1. Open the handout file you cloned from the materials repository: materials/070/nonlinear-equations-handout.qmd (or the Japanese version nonlinear-equations-handout-jp.qmd).

  2. Prime the AI once per chat session. Open materials/AI_TUTOR.md, press Cmd+L / Ctrl+L, and send:

    Read this file. From now on, follow these rules whenever I ask about my handout.

  3. For each question:

    • Highlight the line in the handout that confuses you.

    • Press Cmd+L / Ctrl+L and ask:

      I don’t understand this line. Please add a Q&A block here following AI_TUTOR.md rules.

    • Read the AI’s answer and verify it makes sense.

    • Accept the inserted Q&A block.

  4. Repeat for at least 3 distinct questions.

  5. Render the annotated handout to confirm the Q&A blocks are formatted correctly:

    cd materials/070
quarto render nonlinear-equations-handout.qmd

    Open the generated .html file in your browser and check that the Q&A callout blocks appear.

Note: You do not submit the annotated handout in this repository. The Q&A blocks live inside materials/070/nonlinear-equations-handout.qmd, which is your personal evolving study note.
